Principles

01 · Principles

Working rules

  • Contrast comes from the tonal ramps, never from saturation.
  • Hierarchy is size and space — headings stay at their 500 weight.
  • Elevation is an edge plus ambient darkness, not a heavy shadow.
01 · Principles

Two columns

Quiet symmetry

Two columns with the gutter as the only divider — at this scale the whitespace reads clearly, so no rule is drawn. Each column keeps its flush-left axis and the shared baseline rhythm.

Tonal hierarchy

Heads hold the full text value; the copy drops to the 300 step — contrast from the tonal ramp, the system’s one way of organising attention.

01 · Principles

Three columns

Signal

Three narrow columns for triads — status, stages, teams.

Context

The medium weight labels each column; size stays level.

Action

Short copy only — a line or three; the dark page absorbs the rest.

03

Imagery

03 · Imagery

The lighten treatment

Every photograph runs through the lighten wrapper — dark values sink into the page, light ones keep their shape, so subjects shot on near-black grounds blend in whole.

03 · Imagery

Full bleed

One photograph, edge to edge — the scrim is the ground token rising from the foot, so cover-grade type keeps its contrast without leaving the palette.

03 · Imagery

Half bleed

  • The photograph bleeds to three edges and holds half the stage
  • The copy keeps the content rhythm — same gridlines as every slide
  • Dashes still hang left of the text, whatever sits behind them
03 · Imagery

Mirrored

The flip class changes the photograph’s side and the copy returns to the text axis — one pattern, both directions.
